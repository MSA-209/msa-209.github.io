## Credit Card Industry
- $1.21 trillion outstanding credit card debt in United States [1]
- Grew by 4% from the previous year
- VISA and Mastercard, two biggest credit card transaction networks in the world, have a market cap of $654.8 billion and $467.21 billion respectively [2] [3]
- Chase and American Express, two of the biggest banks, have credit card portfolios valued at $211.12 billion and $149.9 billion respectively in 2023 [4]

## Opportunities for Breakthroughs
- **Simplify rewards and fees for users**
    - Traditional credit cards have hard to use rewards
        - Points system – Hard to convert to currency value
        - Rewards redeemable only in increments (e.g. Wells Fargo had $25 increments)
    - Traditional credit cards have various fees
        - Foreign transaction fees, annual fees, late penalty fees
- **Increase Security**
    - Fraudulent transactions
    - Card skimming - Costs individuals and financial institutions more than $1 billion each year
- **Target Specific Demographics**
    - New to credit
        - 19% of American adults do not have a conventional credit score
        - 28 million adult Americans are credit invisible and 21 million are unscoreable
        - International students or other foreign residents staying within the US on a visa
    - Subprime borrowers
        - 57 million have subprime or below credit score [5]

## Pain Points
- Credit cards exacerbate income inequality.
    - High APRs (20%+) and predatory lending, trap subprime borrowers in debt cycles [6]
- Asymmetric information
    - Traditional FICO models often exclude thin-file borrowers, such as gig workers, potentially leaving them vulnerable to predatory lenders like Credit One [7].
    - Hidden fees: Late fees, foreign transaction fees [8]
    - Rewards: Cardholders don’t fully understand their rewards leading to underutilization [9]

### Why
- **Adverse Selection**
    - Legacy underwriting: Reliance on FICO scores and credit history perpetuates exclusion of creditworthy individuals who don't fit standard mold. [10]
    - Opaque pricing: Fee structures are designed to maximize revenue, rather than providing clear value to the customer. This lack of transparency makes it difficult for consumers to make truly informed decisions. [11]
- **Legacy Systems**
    - Outdated Technology: Core banking and payment network infrastructure hinders innovation, speed, and security. There is a cost involved in updating systems. [12]

## Apple Card
### Background Info
- On August 20, 2019, Apple released the Apple Card to go along with their Apple Pay feature which allows consumers to make transactions digitally using their Apple device.
- Only Apple users can apply for the Apple Card and use its features [13]

### Benefits
- A soft credit inquiry is conducted during the application process, followed by a hard inquiry performed upon acceptance.
- Apple Card Family - Authorized Users at the age of 13.
- **Simplified rewards and fee structure**
    - 2% cashback in daily cash when using Apple Pay; 3% on select merchants
    - You have access to 0% APR monthly installments on any new apple products you purchase.
    - No annual fee, foreign transaction fee, late penalty fee
- **Improved Software**
    - Clean UI that displays weekly spending and balance due.
    - Notifications to pay off statement balance and reduce debt.
- **Physical titanium card with the consumer’s name laser etched onto it.**
    - Has no credit card number on the card for increased security. Virtual card numbers are generated from the wallet app.
    - Amex promoted social status with the Amex Platinum and Gold with their high annual fee and metal credit cards. Apple aims to replicate this by making their cards out of titanium.
    - Also includes a premium unboxing experience like other Apple products.

### Partnership
- Goldman Sachs is an investment bank that primarily deals with corporations, financial institutions, and individuals with large amounts of capital.
    - During the time Apple Card was released, Goldman Sachs was looking to get into consumer finance to expand their portfolio. They are a strong backer for this card.
- Apple Card uses Mastercard as their payment network, which is accepted everywhere.
    - Apple card is World Elite, so it can charge a higher interchange fee to merchants (about 1% higher) [14]

## Market Responses
### Success?
- Since January 2025, there has been more than 12 million current Apple Card users mentioned in an earnings call. [15]
- Goldman Sachs lost $1 billion nine months after the Apple Card unveiled. There has been talks with America Express of replacing Goldman Sachs. [16]
- Recently, Visa offered about $100 million to replace Mastercard as the payment network for the Apple Card, indicating there is still interest in the card. [17]

### Market Responses
- Capital One merges with Discover, valued at $35.3 billion which may introduce new credit cards on the Discover network. [18]
- Capital One frequently updates its mobile app.
- Wells Fargo releases Active Cash card that rewards 2% cashback on all purchases in 2019 as they revamp their credit card lineup.
- Amex releases new card designs like Rose Gold and Boeing 747 for their Platinum card.

## Conclusion
### Apple Card Analysis
- The primary customer base for Apple products consists of people with high credit scores and disposable income
- Customers need to have an Apple device to use the Apple Card
- Although the product is targeted towards subprime borrowers, it attracts the opposite end of the range for credit scores
- It is also not easy to use the Apple Card as a daily driver, the credit card that people use most frequently
    - Limited to Apple Pay transactions
    - Customers will not use this card as often over other cards
- The Apple Card will stay around, but it is not making much profit on its own
    - It is another product offered by Apple

### Did traditional finance companies succeed?
- Credit card industries are, in general, not heavily affected by FinTech card companies
    - People can own multiple credit cards [19]
    - Does not prevent users from holding only the Apple Card
